# Scientific reference status

The composite remains an experimental product index, not a calibrated population rank, clinical score or diagnosis. Coverage describes recorded data, not statistical confidence.

The individual VO2 reference is versioned as friend-2015-treadmill-v1. Kaminsky, Arena and Myers, DOI 10.1016/j.mayocp.2015.07.026, Tables 3 and 4: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC4919021/.

Twelve sex/decade cells cover ages 20-79. The cohort consists of 7,783 tests from eight US exercise laboratories, not a worldwide probability sample. Supported comparisons require direct treadmill CPET, a documented test age/date, peak RER at least 1.0, and a self-reported match to the population without known cardiovascular disease or COPD.

The application reports published threshold intervals or rounded threshold matches. It does not interpolate an exact percentile, assume a Gaussian population distribution, extrapolate extreme tails or manufacture confidence intervals. Standard deviation describes the cohort spread, not an individual's measurement uncertainty. Estimates, unsupported protocols and unknown eligibility remain available for raw tracking.

A newer FRIEND update exists: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/34809986/. Its tables have not been silently mixed into the explicitly versioned 2015 reference. A reference update needs complete primary-table and protocol review.

The calculation report identifies source-backed and provisional inputs separately. Real composite calibration requires a defined target population, permitted protocol-compatible joint participant data, a documented sampling design, frozen normalization and disjoint independent validation with justified uncertainty. Separate unrelated cohorts cannot be spliced into fictitious joint participants.

# TOP1% on a phone

The player should be able to open the app, log one result, see where it lands in the game and leave. Their next visit can start with another test or a session toward a goal. The 36 measurements have three destinations: 13 performance challenges, three bounded tests or comparisons, and 20 optional journal measurements. [Current performance rules](PERFORMANCE_GAME_RULES.md) · [Measurement inventory, units and historical rules](RANKING_METRIC_DECISIONS.md)

## From one test to two

| Action | Test result | Overall game position |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Record a 5 km in 25:00 | Top 10%. A 23:57 run earns the Top 7.5% milestone. | Provisional Top 10% · 1 of 4 areas |
| Choose **Log another** and record a 2:00 dead hang | Top 5%. The next milestone is a 2:30 hang. | **Top 10% → Top 7.07%** · 2 of 4 areas |
| Record another hang of 2:00 | Same individual position | Overall stays at Top 7.07% · 2 areas |

These are worked examples for a 25-year-old player, male reference, weighing 75 kg at the test. The second figure combines running and pulling. Additional observations can move the percentage either way. Each retest replaces that test's contribution while the dated result remains in history.

## The journey

1. **Home.** Quick log offers the 5 km run, grip strength and squat. The main card opens the active goal or offers to repeat the latest dated test. If several goals are active, choose which one to see. **Explore the game scale** opens an optional preview.
2. **Measurements.** Choose one performance test. Search across all measurements to jump directly to a test, health value or functional measure. A player can close the app after any save.
3. **Entry.** Enter the result in its ordinary unit. Times accept minutes and seconds; lifts use load, reps and test-day bodyweight. Use the player's known profile to reduce typing. Optional test details allow changes to age, weight or reference sex for that attempt. A quick entry can carry those facts without claiming an instrument or a verified test protocol.
4. **Result.** Show the individual position, the next attainable result and the overall change. Choose another test, finish, or turn that next result into a dated goal. Show how many of the four areas contribute.
5. **Program.** Choose one or more tests with saved results, set a target for each and choose a date that fits the available training sessions. Record completed, modified or missed sessions and log the benchmark retest separately.
6. **Return.** Record a comparable attempt to see progression. The first-day scenario uses assumed gains. After three fully completed intervals between four comparable dated tests, the personal estimate uses the athlete’s recorded changes. My profile opens the history and ranking views. ## The three measurement groups

| Group | Experience |
| --- | --- |
| **Performance · 13** | Strict push-ups, pull-ups, dips, dead hang, grip, both jumps, bench, squat, deadlift, weighted pull-up, 5 km and 400 m. Each has versioned anchors, direction, profile adjustments and a practical step for the next goal. |
| **Bounded · 3** | Chair stands and eyes-closed balance support functional goals and tracking. A qualifying treadmill VO₂ test supports a published comparison through its source's upper band. A limit on reference precision is independent of the player's personal best. |
| **Health journal · 20** | Preserve the original units, dates, methods and history for recovery, composition, bloodwork and the remaining contextual measures. These observations do not add competition points. |

For grip and countermovement jump a compatible test can also earn a comparison in a named published cohort. The interface identifies that comparison separately from game position. [Reference coverage](PERFORMANCE_TEST_REFERENCES.md)

## What the score means

The overall model uses four areas — pushing, pulling/grip, lower body and running. Within a covered area, each eligible test is counted once. Covered areas have equal weight. The **provisional** label stays until there is at least one valid result in all four. Scoring the two distinct areas above gives the geometric mean of 10 and 5, approximately 7.07%. A balance rule limits extreme differences across areas. [Overall calculation and worked rules](BENCHMARK_CONTRACT.md)

The finest displayed performance result is **Top 0.01% or better**. Athletes can continue improving the underlying measurement. For the reference player in their twenties, 60 clean push-ups earns Top 1%. Bodyweight ratios use the actual weight associated with the lift; the model gives no second credit for that same weight. Missing test-day weight receives a neutral adjustment. The coefficients are deliberate TOP1 game settings. A worldwide position requires a matching representative joint sample with independently supported precision. [Per-test anchors and profile coefficients](PERFORMANCE_GAME_RULES.md)

## Release and next evidence

The web and Capacitor clients share the ranking and entry flow. The useful pilot measures are whether a new player understands the result, saves a second test and comes back for a comparable retest. Review how often each milestone is attained in players who completed the same protocol. Keep changes versioned as measured outcomes become available. A verified leaderboard and a calibrated population percentile each require their own named comparison group and sampling rules.

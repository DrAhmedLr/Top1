# TOP1 game and published comparisons

## Current inventory · 22 November 2026

The journal supports **36 active measurements** and preserves ten archived storage identities. Ranking offers individual TOP1 challenge tiers for **13 directional performance tests**. An improved result can set a new personal best after the last challenge tier. All 36 support a history of numerical observations. Compatible protocols enable meaningful comparisons between retests. See the [metric decisions and effective thresholds](RANKING_METRIC_DECISIONS.md), [published-reference inventory](PERFORMANCE_TEST_REFERENCES.md) and [current product flow](PRODUCT_FOCUS.md).

All 13 open challenges use test-specific rules. The finest displayed game position is Top 0.01% or better. For the reference profile, a 25:00 5 km earns Top 10%. ## Published comparisons

Three active metrics have source-specific published bounds. Each requires a dated result, a compatible age and reference sex, and the matching protocol.

- Direct treadmill CPET uses FRIEND 2022 for ages 20–89. The loaded P10–P90 thresholds give an open Top 10% bound. [FRIEND study](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/34809986/)
- The highest single handgrip from the seated hydraulic protocol uses iGRIPS for ages 20–100+. P5–P95 gives an open Top 5% bound. [iGRIPS study](https://eprints.gla.ac.uk/339600/)
- The Leonardo countermovement jump with arm swing uses the Canadian Health Measures Survey for adults 18–69. The current extraction includes P10/P50/P90/P95 and gives an open Top 5% bound. The separate standing Smedley III two-hand sum is an archived grip identity with data preserved for ages 18–79.

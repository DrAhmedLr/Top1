## Additional reference reviewed

[CDC STEADI chair-stand assessment](https://www.cdc.gov/steadi/media/pdfs/STEADI-Assessment-30Sec-508.pdf) supplies below-average screening thresholds for ages 60–94, not percentile ranks. Its 17-inch chair and final partial-stand counting rule differ from the current full-stand protocol. Added as contextual evidence; no cutoff has been substituted into TOP1 levels.

FRIEND, iGRIPS and CHMS support three active metric comparisons (jump, single-grip and VO₂). The archived combined-grip reference is preserved; exact protocol, dated test age and source cohort are required. The OpenPowerlifting candidate still requires unique-lifter filtering, protocol qualification and uncertainty analysis. New precise global percentiles and success probabilities remain unavailable.

The INSPIRE-T chair-stand study is another candidate for functional comparisons. Before using its thresholds in Ranking, confirm the chair height, hand and completion rules, age and sex cells, sample sizes, cutoffs and data reuse rights. Chair stands currently support personal tracking and functional goals. [INSPIRE-T study](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42822167/)
